# NGSS alignment for science activities

Use when the teacher requests the Next Generation Science Standards or identifies them as the curriculum. Verify the grade band, code, performance expectation, clarification and assessment boundary in the official standard. Consult its evidence statements. If a standard cannot be verified, request its text or describe the alignment as provisional.

In the teacher note, connect the relevant grade-band elements of the science or engineering practice, disciplinary core idea and crosscutting concept to what students actually do together. Name the student work the teacher can inspect and the evidence still missing. Distinguish preparation, partial evidence and a task designed to elicit the performance. Treat mastery as a judgment based on student work.

## Review example

A student predicts equal density after mass and volume both double. This builds understanding of a characteristic property. MS-PS1-2 asks students to interpret property data before and after substances interact to determine whether a chemical reaction occurred. Identify the missing comparison and decision. Retain the original exercise when proportional reasoning is the teacher's goal.

## Design example for MS-PS1-2

Present a classroom investigation with comparable measurements of the starting substances and recovered material under the same conditions. Supply teacher-verified observations of properties within the assessment boundary, such as melting point and solubility. Have students organize before/after data, identify a pattern across properties and argue whether it supports formation of a new substance. Ask which observations could also be explained by a mixture or measurement uncertainty.

The teacher looks for an accurate comparison (analyzing and interpreting data), a link between characteristic properties and formation of substances with different properties (disciplinary ideas), and use of macroscopic patterns as evidence about a change in material composition (crosscutting concept). H5P can record the selected interpretation. Collect the cited observations and reasoning separately when the interaction only records selection. A correct click alone leaves that evidence open.
